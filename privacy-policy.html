Official Policy

Privacy Policy

App: Chess Clock (com.dtsuper3.chessclock)
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for using Chess Clock. This application is designed to function entirely offline as a timer tool for chess matches and board games. Your privacy is critically important to us.

Chess Clock does not collect, track, transmit, or share any personal information.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The app operates completely on-device:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register or sign in to use the application.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, crash data miners, or advertising networks.

3. Device Permissions and Usage

Chess Clock requires minimal hardware capabilities solely to provide timer functionality during gameplay:

  • Screen Wake-Lock: Keeps your display active so the timer does not turn off or lock during a live chess match.
  • Audio & Haptics: Plays tap sound effects and tactile feedback when a player toggles their clock.

No recordings, microphone inputs, or audio transmissions are created or stored.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or tracking libraries.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Chess Clock is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
